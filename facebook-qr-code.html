Turn your Facebook page, group, event, or profile into a scannable shortcut so people can reach the right destination instantly from print, packaging, events, or in-store displays.

What is a Facebook QR code?

A Facebook QR code is a QR code that points to a Facebook destination such as a business page, group, event page, Messenger chat, or public profile. It removes the need for users to type a page name or search through similar results.

Facebook QR codes are strongest when your audience already has high intent and simply needs the fastest path to your page.

Best use cases

Retail and restaurants

Drive customers from a physical location to your page for offers, reviews, or updates.

Events and communities

Promote a Facebook event page or group where attendees can get updates quickly.

Printed marketing

Use flyers, posters, catalogs, and packaging to build followers and engagement.

Customer support

Direct people to Messenger or a service page when quick contact matters.

How to create a Facebook QR code

  1. Copy the exact URL of your Facebook page, group, event, or profile.
  2. Paste that URL into the QR code generator.
  3. Choose a clean style with strong contrast and enough white space.
  4. Test the code on both iPhone and Android before publishing.
  5. Place it where users already have a reason to connect with your brand.

Destination choices

  • Business page for likes, updates, and reviews
  • Group for communities and member discussions
  • Event page for attendance and updates
  • Messenger entry point for customer conversations

Best practices

  • Use the most stable Facebook URL available instead of temporary campaign links.
  • Pair the QR with a clear CTA such as "Follow us on Facebook" or "Join the event".
  • Make sure your Facebook destination looks complete and trustworthy before driving traffic to it.
  • If you may change the destination later, consider a dynamic QR workflow instead of a fixed URL.
