What is a Phone QR Code?

A phone QR code encodes a telephone number. When scanned, it automatically opens the phone app on the user's device and pre-fills your phone number, ready to call. One tap initiates the call.

Perfect for:

  • Business cards and marketing materials
  • Restaurant menus (order by phone)
  • Real estate listings (agent contact)
  • Healthcare offices (book appointment)
  • Field marketing and outdoor campaigns
  • Event flyers and signage

How to Create a Phone QR Code

  1. Enter your phone number: Use international format: +[country code][number]
  2. Example: +1-555-123-4567 or +81-90-1234-5678
  3. Customize: Add colors, logo, or frame
  4. Generate: Create your QR code
  5. Download: Save as PNG, SVG, or PDF
International Format: Always use country code (+1 for US, +44 for UK, +81 for Japan, etc.). This ensures the phone works correctly regardless of where the user is located.

Best Practices for Phone QR Codes

Technical Setup

  • Use international format: Always include country code for global compatibility
  • Test on multiple devices: iOS and Android may handle phone numbers differently
  • Include extension: If applicable: +1-555-123-4567,,,123 (commas add pauses)
  • Verify the number: Test scan and call before printing

Design & Placement

  • Add text CTA: "Tap to call" or "Call us"
  • Use high contrast: Black QR on white or light background
  • Minimum size: 1cm × 1cm for reliable scanning
  • Include number as text: In case QR fails to scan

Business Strategy

  • Set business hours: Ensure someone answers during displayed hours
  • Train staff: Prepare team for calls generated via QR code
  • Track calls: Use call tracking service to measure QR code ROI
  • Multiple locations: Create separate QR codes for different departments/numbers

Troubleshooting

Q: QR code won't scan

A: Ensure high contrast between QR and background. Test on multiple phones. Verify you're using a proper QR scanner app.

Q: Call doesn't go through on desktop

A: Phone QR codes are designed for mobile. Desktop users see a message saying "This requires a phone". This is normal.

Q: How do I track which calls come from QR codes?

A: Use a call tracking service (like CallRail, Invoca, or Twilio). These assign unique numbers to your QR code campaigns.

Q: Can I add a pause between dialing?

A: Yes! Use commas in the number: +1-555-123-4567,,,1 (each comma adds a 2-second pause for extensions).
