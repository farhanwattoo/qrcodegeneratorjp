What is a Google Maps QR code?

A Google Maps QR code links to a specific place, search result, route, pin, or business listing inside Google Maps. It is one of the most practical QR types for local businesses, events, field service teams, and logistics workflows.

Use a Maps QR code when the main goal is arrival, navigation, or confirming the exact destination.

Common uses

  • Storefront posters and business cards
  • Event invitations and venue signage
  • Packaging inserts for showroom visits
  • Delivery, pickup, parking, and wayfinding instructions

How to create the QR code

  1. Open the exact location in Google Maps and copy the share URL.
  2. Paste the URL into the QR generator.
  3. Test the scan on both Android and iPhone mapping flows.
  4. Place the QR where users can stop and scan safely.

If the destination may change, use a dynamic redirect so you do not need to reprint the code later.

Best practices

  • Use the most precise map destination possible.
  • Add supporting text like address, floor, gate, or parking notes near the QR code.
  • Test the code from the real distance and lighting conditions where it will be installed.
  • Keep the final link clean so it opens fast on mobile data.
