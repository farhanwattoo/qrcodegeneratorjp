What is an SMS QR Code?

An SMS QR code encodes a phone number and an optional message. When scanned, it automatically opens the user's default messaging app with the phone number and message pre-populated. They just need to tap "Send."

Perfect for:

  • Marketing campaigns and promotions
  • Event registrations (text to RSVP)
  • Contest entries and voting
  • Customer support (text support number)
  • Lead generation and appointment booking
  • Newsletter signups via SMS

Best Practices for SMS QR Codes

Message Guidelines

  • Keep it short: Under 160 characters (standard SMS length)
  • Be specific: Tell users exactly what to expect: "Text HELLO for a free coupon"
  • Use keywords: Make the message easy to remember: TEXT, ENTER, BOOK, REGISTER
  • Add CTA: Place text near QR: "Text PROMO for exclusive offer"
  • Mobile-friendly: Optimize message for small screens

Legal & Compliance

  • Include opt-in message: "Message & data rates may apply"
  • GDPR/CCPA compliant: Obtain consent before sending SMS
  • Provide unsubscribe: Include "Reply STOP to unsubscribe"
  • Include support contact: List your contact info in messages

Troubleshooting SMS QR Codes

Q: The QR code isn't scanning

A: Ensure adequate white space around the QR code (quiet zone). Test with multiple phones. Make sure contrast is high (dark QR on light background).

Q: Message doesn't appear after scan

A: Your message might be too long or contain special characters. Simplify the text and try again.

Q: How do I track SMS engagement?

A: Use dynamic QR codes which allow you to see scan analytics (device type, location, time). Pair this with SMS platform analytics for complete tracking.

Q: Can I change the message later?

A: With static QR codes, no. Use dynamic QR codes to update the message anytime while keeping the same QR code.
