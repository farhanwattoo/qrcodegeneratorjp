Design Best Practices

  • Background Contrast: Place on light backgrounds, not dark
  • Opacity Levels: 80-90% for visibility, 40-60% for watermarks
  • Padding: Add white/light padding around transparent QR
  • Test Readability: Verify scannable on final background
  • Browser Support: PNG transparency works in all modern browsers
