Product Pages via QR

URL Structure

Short URL: https://shop.com/p/ABC123

Expanded:
https://shop.com/products/item-name?sku=ABC123&source=qr&campaign=emailq4

Parameters:
- source=qr        → Track QR origin
- campaign=emailq4 → Campaign identifier  
- utm_medium=qrcode → Google Analytics

Where QR Codes Appear

  • 📸 Product photos in marketplace (Amazon, eBay)
  • 📦 Physical product packaging
  • 📷 Instagram posts and Stories
  • 📧 Email newsletters
  • 📊 Magazine advertisements
  • 📱 Social media profiles
