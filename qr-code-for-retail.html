Point of Sale Integration

Checkout QR Flow

  1. Customer scans QR at checkout counter
  2. Opens mobile payment interface
  3. Selects payment method
  4. Instant transaction processing
  5. Receipt emailed to customer
Benefit: Reduces checkout time by 40-60%. Customers prefer speed.
