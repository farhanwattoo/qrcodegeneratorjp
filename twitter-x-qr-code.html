Twitter X QR code generator guide

Use a QR code to send people directly to your X profile, a specific post, a thread, or a campaign conversation without asking them to search manually.

What is a Twitter/X QR code?

A Twitter/X QR code is a QR code that opens a profile, post, thread, list, or campaign landing page associated with X. It is useful when your offline audience needs a direct route into a social conversation.

Best use: pair an X QR code with conference signage, product drops, press materials, or creator marketing where real-time conversation matters.

Common use cases

  • Promote a brand or creator profile on physical media
  • Send people to a live launch or event thread
  • Share product updates and announcement posts
  • Drive visitors to a support or feedback conversation

How to create the QR code

  1. Copy the exact X URL for the profile, post, or thread.
  2. Paste it into the generator.
  3. Choose a size that matches the real viewing distance.
  4. Test the scan inside both browser and X app environments.
  5. Use a dynamic redirect if the campaign link may change.

Best practices

  • Use a destination that makes sense without requiring context from another screen.
  • Make sure your post or profile is public and accessible to the intended audience.
  • Place a short CTA near the code so users know what they will get after scan.
  • Prefer thread or profile links over short-lived single-post campaigns when longevity matters.
