What is a TikTok QR code?

A TikTok QR code is a QR code that opens a TikTok profile, video, playlist-style content path, campaign landing page, or linked shop experience. It is especially useful when discovery depends on mobile-native video content.

TikTok QR codes work best when your creative already has a strong reason for the user to continue the story on mobile.

Where to use it

  • Product packaging that leads to demos, tutorials, or creator content
  • Retail displays that connect shoppers to short-form product videos
  • Events and pop-ups that encourage live social participation
  • Printed campaigns that need a stronger entertainment or creator layer

How to create the QR code

  1. Choose the most useful TikTok destination: profile, campaign video, or related landing page.
  2. Paste the URL into the QR generator.
  3. Keep the design simple enough that fast-scrolling mobile users can still scan easily.
  4. Test the final code from actual print or real-size screens.

Best practices

  • Use a CTA like "Watch the tutorial" or "See it in action on TikTok".
  • Link to content that is immediately relevant to the product or placement.
  • Use a dynamic QR workflow if the campaign content will rotate over time.
  • Check that the destination works for users who are not signed in to TikTok.
