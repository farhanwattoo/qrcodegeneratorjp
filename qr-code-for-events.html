Implementation Workflow

Step 1: Generate Unique Tickets

  1. Create ticket inventory (number of attendees)
  2. Generate unique QR codes per ticket
  3. Include attendee data (name, ticket type, section)
  4. Design ticket template with QR code

Step 2: Distribute Tickets

  • Email: Direct ticket delivery with QR code
  • SMS: Text link to mobile wallet
  • Print: Physical tickets for will-call pickup
  • QR Display: Screen-based QR for virtual attendees

Step 3: Check-in at Venue

  1. Deploy QR code scanning app (iPad/tablet)
  2. Scan attendee tickets at entrance
  3. Instant verification + attendance logging
  4. Track real-time headcount and no-shows

Step 4: Engagement During Event

  • Display QR codes on screens/signage
  • Attendees scan for content (slides, photos, announcements)
  • Collect real-time feedback and engagement data
  • Run interactive activities (polls, raffles, scavenger hunts)

Step 5: Post-Event Analytics

  • Export attendance data
  • Analyze engagement patterns
  • Calculate ROI metrics
  • Report to stakeholders and sponsors

Best Practices for Event QR Codes

Ticketing & Check-In

  • Test scanning devices: Verify all QR codes work on chosen scanners
  • Backup systems: Have manual check-in list in case of tech failure
  • Unique codes: Never reuse QR codes between events
  • Expiration dates: Set ticket validity window to prevent fraud
  • Security: Use encryption for sensitive attendee data

Design & Visibility

  • Size matters: Minimum 1cm × 1cm for reliable scanning
  • Contrast: Black QR on white background (avoid color issues)
  • Clear printing: Ensure crisp edges, no pixelation
  • Placement: Bottom center of ticket for easy access
  • Signage: Large QR codes (10cm+) for engagement activities

Analytics & ROI

  • Track engagement: Log every QR code scan with timestamp
  • Attendee journey: Map which sessions/booths each person visited
  • No-show analysis: Identify ticket fraud, estimate future demand
  • Sponsor metrics: Report QR scans by sponsor booth
  • ROI calculation: Measure cost savings vs. digital ticketing vs. printing

Common Challenges & Solutions

Q: Some QR codes won't scan

A: Check phone lighting conditions. Poor lighting causes scan failures. Use larger codes and ensure high contrast.

Q: What if we have internet outages?

A: Use offline-capable scanning apps that sync when connection returns. Maintain printed backup list of attendees.

Q: Can attendees share tickets with others?

A: Yes—use one-time scannable codes or add facial recognition verification for premium events.

Q: How do we prevent ticket fraud?

A: Use unique QR codes per ticket, track scans to prevent duplicates, mark tickets as "used" after first scan.

Q: Can virtual attendees use QR codes?

A: Yes—send them a unique QR code for their screen or create a virtual check-in link that generates a QR code.
