Check-In QR Code Implementation

Flow

  1. Guest receives pre-check-in QR in email/SMS
  2. Scans QR → Mobile check-in interface
  3. Verifies reservation details
  4. Receives digital room key
  5. Direct access to room

Sample QR URL

https://hotel.com/checkin?reservation=ABC123&guest=john.doe&token=x9k2p
Security: Use unique tokens, short expiry times, and HTTPS only for guest information.

Guest Feedback Collection

Post-Check-Out Survey

Display QR on checkout screen linking to:

  • Room cleanliness rating
  • Service quality feedback
  • Suggestion form
  • Future promotion signup

Implementation

QR linking to: https://surveys.hotel.com/feedback?reservation=ABC123

Sample form fields:
- Overall satisfaction (1-5 stars)
- Staff friendliness (1-5 stars)
- Room cleanliness (1-5 stars)
- Would you return? (Yes/No)
- Comments (optional)
- Email for follow-up
