Rounding Techniques

CSS Border-Radius

  • Light Rounding: 5-10px for subtle effect
  • Moderate Rounding: 10-20px for visible softening
  • Extreme Rounding: 30%+ for pill-shape effects
Key Point: Rounding works on the container, not individual QR blocks. The QR data itself stays sharp and scannable.

Design Best Practices

  • Test Scannability: Verify rounded codes scan on all devices
  • Keep Margins: Don't round too aggressively if edges are tight
  • Color Contrast: Maintain high contrast between dark and light areas
  • Consistency: Use same rounding across all codes in a campaign
  • Responsive: Scale rounding proportionally on different sizes
