What is a Location QR Code?

A location QR code encodes GPS coordinates or an address. When scanned, it opens Google Maps, Apple Maps, or the user's default mapping app with your location pre-populated. Perfect for directing people to physical locations.

Perfect for:

  • Event venues and directions
  • Real estate open houses
  • Restaurant and retail locations
  • Parking lot guidance
  • Scavenger hunts and tours
  • Delivery addresses

Best Practices for Location QR Codes

Setup & Accuracy

  • Verify coordinates: Test on Google Maps before printing
  • Use street-level precision: Accurate to building entrance
  • Include parking info: Add on nearby signage
  • Add phone number: Backup if GPS fails

Design & Placement

  • Include address text: Backup for manual entry
  • Add "Find Us" CTA: Clear call to action
  • Map icon: Help users recognize the QR type
  • High contrast: Ensure scanability

Marketing Integration

  • Local SEO: Link QR codes to Google My Business
  • Foot traffic tracking: Use location analytics
  • Combined with other QR types: Location + phone number works great
  • Event promotion: Location QR codes drive attendance

Troubleshooting

Q: Maps open to wrong location

A: Coordinates may be slightly off. Verify GPS coordinates precisely or use address instead of coordinates.

Q: How do I add parking info?

A: Location QR codes just provide coordinates. Add parking instructions in text near the QR code or on your website.

Q: Can users see street view?

A: Yes! Google Maps opens with your location. Users can swipe to street view and explore surroundings before visiting.

Q: Works with offline maps?

A: Yes! If user has downloaded offline maps, location QR codes still work.
